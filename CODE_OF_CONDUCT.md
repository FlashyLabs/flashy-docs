# Code of Conduct

## Our Commitment

We are committed to providing a welcoming and inspiring community for all, regardless of background or identity. This Code of Conduct outlines our expectations for participation in the Flashy community.

## Expected Behavior

- **Be respectful.** Value different viewpoints and experiences.
- **Be inclusive.** Welcome newcomers; help them learn the project norms.
- **Be collaborative.** Work toward shared understanding and solutions.
- **Be patient.** Open source is volunteer-driven; responses may take time.
- **Focus on what is right,** not who is right. Disagreement is normal; attack ideas, not people.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or slurs based on identity
- Deliberate intimidation or threats
- Sustained disruption of community spaces
- Doxxing or publishing others' private information
- Engaging in bad-faith argumentation
- Spamming or commercial solicitation without permission

## Reporting

If you witness or experience behavior that violates this code:

1. **Report to the maintainers.** Email **conduct@flashylabs.com** with details.
2. **Provide context.** Include the incident, date, and any relevant messages.
3. **Confidentiality.** Reports are handled confidentially. The reporter's identity will not be disclosed without permission.

## Enforcement

Violations may result in:
- A request to withdraw or edit a message
- A temporary suspension from community spaces
- A permanent ban from the project

Enforcement decisions are made by project maintainers and are final.

## Acknowledgments

This Code of Conduct is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
